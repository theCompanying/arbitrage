# LLC Formation Guide

**Status**: Ready to execute  
**Time**: 1-2 hours (spread over few days)  
**Cost**: $50-200 (state filing fees)  
**Timeline**: 1-5 days for LLC, 1 day for EIN  

---

## Why Form an LLC?

- **Liability protection**: Personal assets protected from business debts
- **Tax flexibility**: Pass-through taxation by default
- **Professional credibility**: More trustworthy to suppliers/customers
- **Banking**: Required for business bank account
- **Amazon Seller**: Business account benefits (lower fees, more features)

---

## Step 1: Choose State of Formation (15 min)

**Options**:
1. **Home State** (recommended for simplicity)
   - Pros: No registered agent needed, simpler compliance
   - Cons: May have higher fees than Delaware

2. **Delaware** (most popular for businesses)
   - Pros: Business-friendly courts, privacy, low franchise tax
   - Cons: Need registered agent (~$100/year), foreign qualification in home state

3. **Wyoming** (privacy-focused)
   - Pros: Low fees, strong privacy laws
   - Cons: Less established case law than Delaware

**Recommendation**: Start with home state for simplicity unless planning to raise VC funding.

---

## Step 2: Choose LLC Name (15 min)

**Requirements**:
- Must include "LLC" or "Limited Liability Company"
- Cannot use restricted words (Bank, Insurance, etc.)
- Must be unique in state

**Check availability**:
- Go to state's Secretary of State website
- Search business name database
- Reserve name if needed (~$10-50)

**Name suggestions**:
- [YourBrand] Arbitrage LLC
- [YourBrand] Trading LLC
- [YourBrand] Commerce LLC

---

## Step 3: File Articles of Organization (30-60 min)

**Methods**:

### Option A: State Website (Cheapest)
1. Go to state's Secretary of State website
2. Find "Business Services" or "Corporations Division"
3. File "Articles of Organization" or "Certificate of Formation"
4. Pay filing fee ($50-200 depending on state)
5. Processing: 1-5 business days (expedited available)

### Option B: LegalZoom (~$100 + state fees)
1. Go to legalzoom.com
2. Select "LLC Formation"
3. Complete questionnaire
4. LegalZoom files for you
5. Processing: 5-10 business days

### Option C: IncFile (~$0 + state fees)
1. Go to incfile.com
2. Free formation service
3. Only pay state fees
4. Upsells available (decline if not needed)
5. Processing: 3-5 business days

**Information Needed**:
- LLC name
- Principal address
- Registered agent name & address
- Management structure (member-managed or manager-managed)
- Organizer name (you)

---

## Step 4: Get EIN from IRS (15 min)

**EIN (Employer Identification Number)**: Free, required for banking

1. Go to https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online
2. Click "Apply Online Now"
3. Complete application:
   - Legal name: Your LLC name
   - Trade name: (optional)
   - Mailing address
   - Responsible party: Your SSN
   - Reason for applying: Started new business
4. Submit and receive EIN immediately

**Note**: Online application available Mon-Fri, 6am-10pm ET

---

## Step 5: Operating Agreement (30 min)

**Required?** Depends on state, but highly recommended

**What it covers**:
- Member ownership percentages
- Voting rights
- Profit/loss distribution
- Management structure
- Procedures for adding/removing members

**Template sources**:
- LegalZoom (~$99)
- RocketLawyer (~$40)
- Free templates: docracy.com, lawinsider.com

**Single-member LLC**: Can use simple template

---

## Step 6: Open Business Bank Account (1-2 hours)

**Required Documents**:
- Articles of Organization (approved)
- EIN confirmation letter (IRS)
- Operating Agreement
- Personal ID (driver's license)
- Personal SSN (for signature card)

**Bank Options**:
- **Traditional**: Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo
- **Online**: Novo, Mercury, Bluevine (recommended for startups)
- **Credit Unions**: Often lower fees

**Recommended**: Mercury or Novo (free, startup-friendly, online)

---

## Step 7: Sales Tax Permit (30 min)

**Required if**: Selling physical goods in most states

1. Go to state's Department of Revenue website
2. Apply for "Seller's Permit" or "Sales Tax License"
3. Provide: EIN, LLC info, business address
4. Free in most states
5. File periodic returns (monthly/quarterly/annual)
