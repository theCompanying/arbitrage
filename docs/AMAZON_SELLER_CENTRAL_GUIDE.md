# Amazon Seller Central Account Setup Guide

**Status**: Ready to execute  
**Time**: 30-45 minutes  
**Approval wait**: 1-2 days (up to 2 weeks for full verification)  
**Critical**: Cannot launch without this account  

---

## Prerequisites

- Government-issued ID (driver's license or passport)
- Credit card (for seller fees)
- Bank account (for disbursements)
- Business information (or personal for Individual plan)
- Phone number (for verification)

---

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Start Registration (5 min)

1. Go to https://sell.amazon.com/
2. Click "Sign up" or "Start selling"
3. Choose account type:
   - **Individual**: $0.99 per sale (recommended to start)
   - **Professional**: $39.99/month (unlimited sales)

**Recommendation**: Start with Individual, upgrade after first successful launch.

### 2. Create/Login to Amazon Account (5 min)

- Use existing Amazon customer account OR create new one
- Use business email if available
- Enable two-factor authentication (recommended)

### 3. Provide Business Information (10 min)

**For Individual Plan**:
- Legal name (matches ID)
- Address
- Phone number

**For Professional Plan (LLC)**:
- Business name
- Business address
- EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- Business phone

### 4. Identity Verification (10 min)

Upload clear photos of:
- Government-issued ID (front and back)
- Credit card (front, hide middle digits)
- Bank statement or utility bill (for address verification)

### 5. Billing & Disbursement (10 min)

**Credit Card** (for seller fees):
- Enter valid credit card
- Amazon may charge small verification amount

**Bank Account** (for disbursements):
- Routing number
- Account number
- Account type (checking/savings)
- Bank name

### 6. Store Setup (5 min)

- Choose seller display name
- Set up return address
- Configure shipping settings (FBA vs. FBM)
- Select product categories to sell in

### 7. Submit & Wait for Approval

- Review all information
- Submit application
- Approval timeline:
  - Basic approval: 1-2 days
  - Full verification: up to 2 weeks

---

## Post-Approval Actions

1. **Complete FBA Settings**:
   - Inbound shipping preferences
   - Return address
   - Prep requirements

2. **List First Product**:
   - Create product listing
   - Set pricing
   - Choose FBA or FBM fulfillment

3. **Send Inventory to Amazon** (if using FBA):
   - Create shipment plan
   - Print FBA labels
   - Ship to Amazon warehouse

---

## Fees

**Individual Plan**:
- $0.99 per item sold
- Referral fee: 8-15% (category dependent)
- FBA fees: based on size/weight

**Professional Plan**:
- $39.99/month
- No per-item fee
- Additional selling tools included

## Troubleshooting

**Verification Delays**:
- Ensure all documents are clear and readable
- Information must match exactly (name, address)
- Respond promptly to any Amazon emails

**Account Rejection**:
- Common reasons: unclear documents, mismatched info
- Can reapply with corrected information
- Contact Seller Support for specific rejection reason
